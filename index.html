PageForge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The short version

PageForge works on your phone. Your documents are never uploaded to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no history, and no analytics. The app shows ads from Google AdMob to stay free.

1. Who we are

PageForge is an Android app that lets you scan, merge, split, compress, convert, sign and edit documents and photos. In this policy, "we" means the developer of PageForge. Contact: mrityunjaykumar579@gmail.com

2. Your files and documents

  • All processing (merging, compressing, converting, OCR, signing and so on) happens on your device. We do not receive, see, or store your files on any server.
  • When a job finishes, the result is kept in the app's private storage for 10 minutes so you can download it again, and is then deleted automatically.
  • Only when you tap Download is a copy saved to the Downloads/PageForge folder on your phone. Files you download are yours to keep or delete.
  • The app does not keep a list of your recent files, saved signatures, passwords, or any document history. Temporary working files are removed when a job ends and when the app starts.

3. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet / network state: only to load ads and to let Google's on-device tools download their language or scanner models. Your documents are not sent over the internet by the app.
  • Camera: used only when you choose Scan or QR scan, through Google's on-device document scanner and code scanner. Pictures are processed on your device.
  • The app does not ask for contacts, location, microphone, SMS, or broad access to your storage. Files are picked using Android's file picker, so the app only sees what you choose.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

PageForge shows a small banner ad and occasional full-screen ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to show ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some other regions), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later from About & privacy → Ad privacy choices.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Google on-device tools

Some features use Google ML Kit (text recognition, face detection, background removal, document scanner, code scanner). These run on your device. Google Play services may download model files and may collect basic technical data as described in the ML Kit data disclosure.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location or documents.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not run our own analytics or tracking.
  • We do not back up app data to the cloud (the app has none to back up).

7. Children

PageForge is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Because your files stay on your device and are not sent to us, the main protection is your phone's own security. Results held for 10 minutes sit in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
